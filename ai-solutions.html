What we can build with AI

Every build is bespoke and trained on your own data — this is the range of what we ship. Each does real work from day one.

AI receptionist

Answers calls, live chat, WhatsApp and DMs around the clock — books appointments, answers your FAQs from your own info, and qualifies leads before they reach you. Great for clinics, salons, home services and any business that loses enquiries after hours.

AI gift picker

A short, friendly quiz that turns an unsure shopper into a confident buyer — a few questions about the occasion and budget, then the perfect product from your catalogue. Lifts conversion and average order value on gifting-heavy stores.

AI product recommender

Personalizes what each visitor sees and suggests the right add-ons at the right moment — like your best salesperson working every page at once. Plugs into your existing storefront and learns from real behaviour, not guesswork.

AI document extractor

Turns invoices, purchase orders, forms, receipts and IDs into clean, structured data that flows straight into your systems — no manual typing, no copy-paste errors. Pays for itself fastest in finance, logistics and back-office teams.

AI booking & scheduler

Fills your calendar in natural conversation, handles reschedules and cancellations, and sends smart reminders that cut no-shows — synced to the calendar and tools you already use. Ideal wherever appointments drive revenue.

AI review responder

Drafts on-brand, on-tone replies to every Google, app-store or marketplace review in seconds, and flags the ones that genuinely need a human. Protects your reputation and keeps response times low without a dedicated team.

AI support chatbot

Trained strictly on your own docs, help articles and policies, it answers customers instantly inside your product or site — and hands off cleanly to a human when it should. Deflects repetitive tickets so your team handles the ones that matter.

Questions we get asked

Where does my data go, and is it private?

Your data stays yours. We build on your own infrastructure and databases wherever possible, and when we use a model provider we send only what a task needs — never your whole dataset for training. We scope data handling, retention and access with you up front, so you know exactly what leaves your systems and what doesn't.

Which AI model do you use?

Whichever fits the job — we're not tied to one vendor. We pick per use case based on accuracy, cost, speed and privacy, and design so a model can be swapped later without a rewrite. The goal is the outcome you need, not a logo.

Will this replace my staff?

Usually it removes the repetitive work — after-hours enquiries, data entry, first-line questions — so your team spends time on the things that actually need a person. Most clients use it to handle volume they couldn't staff for, not to cut the people they have.

Does it plug into what I already run?

Yes. These are built to bolt onto your existing website, store, inbox, CRM or calendar — no rip-and-replace. If a tool has an API, we integrate with it; if it doesn't, we find the cleanest bridge.

How fast can I see something working?

A focused first version of a single AI capability is typically a couple of weeks to something you can use, then we iterate. You see a real, working build every Friday — so scope stays honest and you're never waiting months to find out if it works.

Do I own it?

Yes. The code ships to your repository and runs on your servers, and the data lives in your own database. No per-seat AI licence and no vendor who can switch you off.
